# 1. Phenomenon

Point at it: a table, a column, a figure that puzzled, a thing seen in the market. One sentence, in the user's words,
without a hypothesis. "Returns pile up in some weeks and not in others" is a phenomenon; "discounted orders come back
more often" is already an event and a question, and goes to steps 6 and 7.

Writes `title` and `phenomenon`.

# 5. Graph

Before any conditional question, say what may be conditioned on. If the project has a causal model of its domain,
point at it and name the nodes the variables stand for; otherwise sketch the arrows in words: what is decided before
what, what is a root nothing in the data causes (the hour of day, the weekday).

Then list, in words, the conditionings the graph forbids and why: a collider (a common effect of A and B: conditioning
on it makes them look related) and a mediator (on the path from B to A: conditioning on it hides the relation). A
question that conditions on one of these is still asked, but the page says what its answer cannot mean.

The graph says nothing about causes here: P(A | B) outside the null means B tells you about A, not that B makes A.
